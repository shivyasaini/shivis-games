Ambervale Notice Board

DRAGON SIGHTED!Shepherds swear a great red dragon roosts above the clouds at Eagle's Perch. Some say it lets the brave climb on its back. Ride it once and it will answer your call (press G) for ever after.
Seven Lost BladesSeven blades of the old kingdom still shine. Follow the golden beams of light that rise into the sky — each one marks a blade waiting to be found.
A Bone-Hilted SwordInside the great skull of the fallen dragon a sword rests on a stone pedestal. Whoever lifts it strikes twice as hard.
The Warden's KeepThe Warden of the Hollow Keep bars his doors to all. Bring him down and the keep's three grand floors — hall, library, armoury and chambers — are yours to explore. His guards will fall with him.
FIGHTERS WANTEDThe Grand Arena seeks challengers! Ring the bronze gong on the sand. Gladiators, wolves, boars, ogres and trolls await — tougher every two levels. Knocked out? Just try again. (Teleport there from your map.)
Fresh Fish BoughtStand by any lake, river or pond and press E to cast. Marta the Fishmonger by the square pays gold for your basket. Golden Carp are rare; Moonfish only bite at night. Sturdy rods and Golden Lures sold here.
Doors Always OpenEvery home in Ambervale and the frost village welcomes travellers — walk up and press E at a door to step inside and warm yourself by the fire.
Mountain LookoutsSeven mountain roads begin at the main roads and climb to stone lookouts with benches and grand views. Look for the gold lines on your map. At Dawnwatch it is always a moonlit night.
FrostspireA winding road climbs the frozen peak past ice crystals and frozen trees. Mind the glassy ice on the road — it's slippery!
Horse RidingLunging pen and show-jumping arena just outside the village. Ride at any fence at a trot to jump it. Thirsty horses drink at lakes and troughs. Press H to call your horse.
Wells & WaterA cool drink from any well restores your health and strength. Wade through ponds and lakes — but beware the wet face!
Odd PlacesTravellers whisper of a field of swords where night never ends, a drowned cathedral, gallows in a black wood, a bell tower you can climb, and a castle with a spiral stair to its very top.
A Castle of Your OwnDefeat the Warden of the Hollow Keep, then sit in the lord's high seat at the head of the great hall and press E to claim the keep. Your blue-and-gold banners will fly from its towers and it becomes Your Castle on the map.
Weary TravellersEvery bed you find — in village homes, forest lodges and the keep's bedchamber — is yours to use. Press E beside one to sleep until morning and wake fully healed.
A Secret WordSome say there is a secret code that opens strange doors. Those who know it keep it close. Look for "Secret Code" in the pause menu.
